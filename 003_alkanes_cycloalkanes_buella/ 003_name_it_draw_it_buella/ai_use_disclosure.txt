AI USE DISCLOSURE

Course: CHEM 13 – Organic Chemistry (Lecture) Activity: Asynchronous
Activity No. 3 – Name It, Draw It

AI tool used: ChatGPT (OpenAI)

Purpose of use: ChatGPT was used as a learning and writing support tool
to explain IUPAC nomenclature concepts, help decode the given names in
Part B, check the expected molecular formulas, and organize the
explanations into concise text for the activity cards.

Naming and structure verification: The Alkane (https://thealkane.com/)
was used as required by the activity to draw/check the molecular
structures and obtain returned names. The AI-generated explanations were
treated as assistance, not as the final authority. The structures,
returned names, formulas, and final card contents must be checked
against the activity instructions and the student’s own work.

Student responsibility: I am responsible for reviewing the information,
correcting any errors, and ensuring that the submitted structures,
names, formulas, and explanations are accurate. I will disclose any
additional tools used if applicable.
